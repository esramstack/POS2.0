Six steps

The whole setup, honestly listed

Most owners do this themselves on a quiet morning. If you prefer company, book a free onboarding call.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email, name your business and pick your industry. This shapes sensible defaults so you start close to done.

2

Add your products

Type them, import a spreadsheet, or scan barcodes as you go. Categories, prices and cost prices, cost prices unlock live profit.

3

Set up staff and permissions

Add your team, assign roles and decide who can void, discount and refund. Accountability starts before the first sale.

4

Connect your hardware

Standard receipt printers, cash drawers, barcode scanners and supported scales connect in minutes. Or start with just a tablet.

5

Take a test sale

Run a transaction end to end: sell, pay, print, refund. When the test receipt looks right, you are ready.

6

Go live

Open the doors. Your dashboard starts filling with real numbers immediately, and support is a message away if anything snags.

Questions

Before you begin

Do I need special hardware?

No. POS 2.0 runs on standard modern tablets and PCs, with common ESC/POS receipt printers. Use what you have, add what you need.

Can I import products from my old system?

Yes. Export from your current system to a spreadsheet and use our import template. Onboarding can help map the columns.

Can I practice before going live?

Yes. Run test transactions freely and clear them before opening. Your real reporting starts clean.

What if I get stuck?

Help and support covers guides, chat and email, and every plan includes the free onboarding call.
