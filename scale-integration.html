What this replaces

How it works

Scale integration in practice

Place the item. The price appears.

Connect a supported scale and weight flows straight into the transaction at the product’s per-unit price. Tare, price-per-100g and per-kg configurations are all supported.

✓Direct weight capture from connected scales
✓Per-gram, per-100g and per-kg pricing
✓Tare handling for containers and packaging
✓Weighed and barcoded items on the same bill

Accuracy you can prove.

Every weighed line records the captured weight, so receipts and reports show exactly what was measured, priced and paid. An exclusive most POS systems in this class do not offer.

Which scales are supported?

Common counter scales with standard serial or USB output. Our team confirms your model during setup, see help and support.

Can one till serve weighed and normal products?

Yes, weighed lines and barcode lines mix freely on the same transaction.

Does it handle tare weights?

Yes, per-container tare values subtract automatically before pricing.
