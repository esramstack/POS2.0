Questions

Frequently asked

Does a sale in one branch update all branches?

Stock is tracked per branch, and totals update everywhere instantly, so head office and the counter always agree.

Can it manage recipes and ingredients?

Yes. Selling a finished item deducts its components, which keeps food cost and stock counts honest.

How do transfers between branches work?

A transfer is requested, dispatched and received in the system, with quantities confirmed at both ends and a full trail.

What if stock is adjusted manually?

Adjustments require a reason and are logged by user, so every change to the number is accountable.
