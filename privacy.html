Overview

This policy explains what data POS 2.0 ("we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, applications and services. We aim to state this plainly: we collect what is needed to run the service, we do not sell personal data, and your business data belongs to you.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone and business details you provide at sign-up.
  • Operational data: products, sales, inventory, staff and customer records you create by using the service.
  • Usage data: logs, device information and diagnostics that help us keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Support data: messages and files you share when contacting us.

How we use data

  • To provide, maintain and improve the service.
  • To secure accounts, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To communicate service updates, and marketing only where you have consented.
  • To produce aggregate, de-identified statistics that never identify a business or person.

Your customers’ data

Customer records you collect through CRM and loyalty features are your data. We process them on your instructions to run your programs, and never use them to market to your customers ourselves. You are responsible for collecting this data lawfully in your market.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate (hosting, communications, payments), under contracts limiting their use; with authorities where the law requires; or in a corporate transaction, with notice to you. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account is active. After cancellation you may export your data; we delete or de-identify it after the retention window described in our terms, except where law requires longer.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is role-based, logged and reviewed. Support access to your account occurs only with your permission per session and is visible to you.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete personal data. Contact us through the contact page and we will respond within the time your law requires.

Changes and contact

We will notify you of material changes to this policy. Questions can be sent through the contact page.
