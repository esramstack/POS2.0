How it works

Staff and commissions in practice

Every sale has a name on it.

Each transaction is attributed to the staff member who handled it. Shift reports show sales, average basket, discounts given and voids, per person, automatically.

✓Individual performance report per shift
✓Sales, basket size and discount rate per person
✓Hourly attribution for roster planning
✓Leaderboards if you want them, private if you do not

Commission calculated by the system, not the argument.

Define rules once: flat rate, tiered, per category or per target. The system computes payable commission continuously, and month end becomes a print button.

✓Flat, tiered and target-based commission rules
✓Category and product-level rates
✓Real-time commission visibility per staff member
✓Role-based permissions and full override audit trail

Questions

Frequently asked

Can staff see their own commission?

That is your choice. You can give each person live visibility of their own numbers, or keep it management-only.

How granular are permissions?

Per role and per action: voids, refunds, discounts above a threshold, price changes, report access and more.

Does it integrate with payroll?

Commission and hours export cleanly for your payroll process. Enterprise plans can discuss direct integrations.

Can one person work across two branches?

Yes, a staff profile can be assigned to multiple branches with attribution kept per location.
