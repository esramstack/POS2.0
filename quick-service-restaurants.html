How fast is order entry really?

Most items are one tap, combos are one tap with automatic pricing. Cashiers reach full speed within a shift.

Can it drive a kitchen display instead of printers?

Yes, kitchen display and printers are both supported and can run together, routed by station.

Do delivery platform orders flow in automatically?

Through our integrations, orders can land directly in the queue. See the Integrations page for currently supported platforms in your market.

Can head office lock menus and prices?

Yes. Menus, prices and offers are controlled centrally and pushed to every outlet instantly.
