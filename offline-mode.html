Questions

Frequently asked

How long can it run offline?

Days if needed. Local storage comfortably holds heavy trading volumes until reconnection.

What about card payments offline?

Card processing depends on your payment provider’s offline rules. Cash and recorded payment types continue regardless.

Do reports work offline?

Local till reports do. Consolidated multi-branch dashboards resume once branches reconnect.

How do I know sync completed?

A sync indicator shows pending and completed counts, and the daily close confirms reconciliation.
