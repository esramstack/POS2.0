Questions

Frequently asked

Can branch managers change prices?

Only if you allow it. Pricing control is a permission you grant per role, per branch.

How does stock work across branches?

Stock is tracked per branch with transfers between them. See inventory management for the full workflow.

Can I compare branches fairly?

Yes, same metrics, same definitions, side by side, with per-branch P&L rather than just revenue.

Is there a limit to how many branches?

No practical limit. From two locations to national chains, the model is the same. For large fleets see enterprise pricing.
