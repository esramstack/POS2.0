Questions

Frequently asked

Does it handle size and colour variants?

Yes, matrix products with per-variant barcodes, stock and reporting are standard.

Can I run promotions and markdowns centrally?

Yes. Discounts, multi-buys and scheduled markdowns are set once and applied across selected stores.

What about serial numbers for electronics?

Serialised inventory is supported, so warranty and returns trace to the exact unit sold.

How are returns controlled?

Role-based permissions, reason codes and a complete audit trail on every return, exchange and override.
