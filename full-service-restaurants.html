Questions

Frequently asked

Does it handle course timing and holds?

Yes. Servers can hold courses and fire them when the table is ready. The kitchen sees the sequence, not a wall of tickets.

Can we keep our existing receipt printers?

In most cases yes. POS 2.0 works with standard ESC/POS thermal printers. Our team confirms compatibility during setup.

What happens if the internet drops during service?

Nothing visible. Orders, bills and payments continue in full offline mode and sync when the connection returns.

Does inventory track recipes or just items?

Both. Sell a dish and the system deducts its ingredients, so food cost and stock counts stay honest.
