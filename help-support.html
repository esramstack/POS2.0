Common questions

Quick answers

How do I reset my password?

Use the reset link on the login page. If your email has changed, contact support with your business details to verify ownership.

My printer stopped printing. What first?

Check power and cable, confirm the printer shows in device settings, and print a test page. The hardware guide covers the five causes behind 90% of cases.

How do I close the day correctly?

Run the daily close from the register: count cash against the expected figure, confirm card totals and post the close. The report emails automatically.

Can support access my account?

Only with your explicit permission per session, and every support access is logged and visible to you.

What are support hours?

Email support on all plans with responses within one business day. Growth adds priority chat, Enterprise has 24/7 coverage with contractual SLAs.

Still stuck? Talk to a person.

Write to support with what you tried, and we will reply with an answer, not a link back to this page.
